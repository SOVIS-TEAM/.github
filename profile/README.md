# SOVIS

A student team at Dongguk University in Seoul. We make **SoundVisualizer**, an open-source app that shows the direction, loudness and type of sounds on screen for deaf and hard-of-hearing people.

**[Website](https://sovis-team.github.io/en/) · [Android demo](https://youtu.be/Rl8BLXjoTv4) · [Windows demo](https://youtu.be/NBRV5AWpb38) · [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/soundvisualizer.official/) · [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@soundvisualizer.official) · [한국어](#한국어)**

## Projects

| Project | Platform | License |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [SoundVisualizer for Android](https://github.com/SOVIS-Team/SoundVisualizer-Android) | Android 10+ | Apache-2.0 |
| [SoundVisualizer for Windows](https://github.com/SOVIS-Team/SoundVisualizer-Windows) | Windows 10/11 (64-bit) | AGPL-3.0 |

- Draws the sound a phone or PC is playing as a glow along the screen edges, on top of videos, music and games.
- An on-device AI sorts sounds into ambient, human sounds and danger. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
- Tested with deaf students at the Seoul National School for the Deaf (May and October 2026).
- Honorable Mention, Digital Inclusion track, 2026 Disability Hackathon (Korea Foundation for Persons with Disabilities).
